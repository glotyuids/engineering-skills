# Pack: Writing

Product and long-form writing that earns its place: the purposeful-writing method, honesty gates for anything generative, and the cover-letter craft and review rubric.

## Skills

- [`purposeful-text-writing`](purposeful-text-writing/SKILL.md)
- [`writing-honesty-gates`](writing-honesty-gates/SKILL.md)
- [`cover-letter-craft`](cover-letter-craft/SKILL.md)
- [`review-cover-letter`](review-cover-letter/SKILL.md)

## Expects

Nothing.

## Soft companions

`lang-ru` — companions are suggestions, not requirements. Every skill here works with the
companion pack absent; cross-pack references are written to degrade gracefully.

## Notes

`purposeful-text-writing` is the generic method; a product's own voice, vocabulary and banned words belong in a local product skill layered on top of it — the same generic/project split the rest of the library uses.
